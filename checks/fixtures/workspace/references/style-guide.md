# Style guide

Notes on how the weeknotes should feel.

## Register

Keep it grounded. The post should sound like a person who was there, not like a
report about a person who was there.

## Length

Long enough to be worth reading, short enough to finish standing up.

## Structure

Some weeks have a shape and some do not. Where there is a shape, follow it.
Where there is not, put the most interesting thing first and let the rest fall
where it falls.

## A note on tone

Do not oversell. Do not undersell either.
