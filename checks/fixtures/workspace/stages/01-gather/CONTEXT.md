# 01 gather - collect the week's material

One job: pull the week's raw notes into one ordered file.

## Inputs

| Source | File/Location | Section/Scope | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Working | `../../notes/week.md` | Full file | the raw material |

## Process

1. Read the raw notes.
2. Order them by day.
3. Drop anything that is not about the work.

## Outputs

| Artifact | Location | Format |
|---|---|---|
| Ordered week | `output/gathered.md` | markdown |

## Human check

Read the ordered week. Delete anything that should not be public. The next
stage reads whatever is left here.
