# Findings, June

- Active accounts fell from 1180 to 1161 across the month, a drop of 19 (1.6%).
- Tickets raised rose from 204 to 262 per week, up 58 (28%).
- Breached tickets rose from 3 to 14 per week. The contractual threshold is 10
  per week and it was crossed in week 4 for the first time this year.
- The account team has not been told about the breach threshold crossing.
