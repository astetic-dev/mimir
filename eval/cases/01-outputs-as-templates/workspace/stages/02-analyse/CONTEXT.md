# 02 analyse - work out what the month means

One job: turn the tidied figures into findings.

## Inputs

| Source | File/Location | Section/Scope | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Working | `../01-collect/output/collected.md` | Full file | the figures |
| Reference | `../../references/report-standards.md` | "Required content" | what has to be covered |

## Process

1. Read the figures.
2. Compare against the previous month.
3. Note every threshold crossed.

## Outputs

| Artifact | Location | Format |
|---|---|---|
| Findings | `output/analysis.md` | markdown |

## Human check

Read the findings. Add anything you know that the figures do not show.
