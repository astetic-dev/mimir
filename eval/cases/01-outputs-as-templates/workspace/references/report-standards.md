# Report standards

What a monthly client report has to do.

## Purpose

The report tells the client what happened to their account this month and what
it means. It is read by one person who is busy.

## Required content

Every report contains, somewhere:

- the month's headline number and how it moved against the previous month
- any threshold that was crossed, in either direction
- anything the client has to decide before the next report

## Evidence

Every claim about a number carries the number. A movement is given as both the
absolute change and the percentage.

## Length

Two pages at most. A busy reader who stops after the first paragraph should
still know whether anything needs their attention.

## Tone

Direct. The client is paying for judgement, not for hedging. Where the data does
not support a judgement, say that instead of softening one.
